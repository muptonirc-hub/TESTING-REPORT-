    AI settings

    Drafting interpretations and reading scanned notes or VALD screenshots use Claude through the clinic’s own Claude API key.

    The key is separate from a Claude subscription and is paid per use: roughly 1–2 cents per drafted interpretation and a few cents per scanned photo or screenshot.

    1. Sign in to the Claude Console at platform.claude.com.
    2. Under Settings › Billing, buy some credit and set a monthly spend limit.
    3. Under Settings › API keys, create a key, copy it and paste it here.

    The key is saved only on this device and is only sent to Anthropic when you use Draft with AI or Scan notes.
